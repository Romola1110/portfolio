01 · Origin

缘起：我想亲手造一个「完全属于自己的东西」

Origin: I wanted something entirely my own

From distant reading to a living scroll

From distant reading to a living scroll

故事不是从「会不会写代码」开始的。它开始于港中文研究生课上的一次文本分析——我用 R 语言去远读《都柏林人》，看情感值如何在短篇里起伏，像看天气图一样看文字的气压。那一刻我忽然觉得：文学研究不必只停在批注与段落，它也可以变成可触摸的界面、可漫游的路径。

It began not with “knowing how to code,” but with distant reading in an MA seminar — watching Joyce’s Dubliners rise and fall as data weather.

The story did not begin with “knowing how to code.” It began in a graduate seminar at CUHK, when I used R for distant reading of Dubliners—watching emotion scores rise and fall across stories like a weather map of feeling. Suddenly literary study need not stop at annotations; it could become a path you wander, an interface you touch.

兴趣像一粒种子：我想试试 AI 辅助编程与部署——不是让机器替我「生成一个作品集模板」，而是我当主编，AI 当夜班排字工。我写文案、定视觉、挑诗句、画信息架构；它帮我把藤蔓曲线、启封动画、移动端点击这些琐碎针脚缝上。改到第无数版时，我仍记得某次为了让启封信在手机上「站在画面正中」，来回调了整晚——因为我想让第一眼就有「被人郑重递来一封信」的感觉。

Curiosity became a seed: I wanted to try AI-assisted coding and deployment—not to let a model spit out a template portfolio, but to stay editor-in-chief while AI worked the night shift of typesetting. I wrote copy, set the visual system, chose poems, designed information architecture; it helped stitch vine curves, unfold animations, mobile taps. On one night I kept adjusting the opening letter until it stood centred on a phone screen—because the first glance should feel like receiving a letter, not a dashboard.

一笺为引，一卷为心；同声相应，与君共寻。

A small scroll of light, offered as an invitation — when kindred voices answer, we walk this way together.

— 网站启封页题辞 · Opening motto

02 · Theme

主题心法：四时风物，让时间成为首页

Theme: Let time itself be the homepage

Seasons · Solar terms · Poems in two tongues

Seasons · Solar terms · Poems in two tongues

「四时风物」不是装饰性的国风皮肤。我想回答的是：一个人文背景的人，如何用网站讲述时间如何被感知——春有花信，夏有长风，秋有落木，冬有归藏。导航旁的日晷会显示当日节气与一句节气诗；背景藤蔓与蝴蝶随季节变色；启封页漂浮着中英诗片。你看到的不是静态海报，而是一座会呼吸的小园子。

“Four Seasons Scenery” is not decorative chinoiserie. I wanted to ask how someone trained in the humanities might let a website narrate how time is felt—spring’s blossom-news, summer’s long wind, autumn’s leaving leaves, winter’s returning stillness. A sundial beside the nav shows today’s solar term and a short verse; vines and butterflies shift with the season; the opening letter floats bilingual poem-fragments. Not a static poster—a little garden that breathes.

风传花信，雨濯春尘。

The wind carries word of blossoms; spring washes the world anew.

暮色含金，木叶将辞。

Dusk gilds the world in gold; the leaves prepare to leave.

— 春季主标题之一 · One of the season headlines — An autumn headline from the site

03 · How to wander

漫游指南：请像读一封信那样点开

How to wander: Open it like a letter

A gentle path from seal to seasons

A gentle path from seal to seasons

启

第一步 · 启封

Step 1 · Unfold

进入网站，先停在「信纸」前。可读中英题辞，也可点右上角春夏秋冬小圆钮预览季节气质，再点 啟封 · Unfold。那一下，是我最在意的仪式感——像把信封口轻轻撕开。

You arrive at a letter-paper. Read the bilingual motto, or tap the tiny season dots to preview the mood, then press 啟封 · Unfold. That click is the ritual I cared about most—like easing open an envelope.

晷

第二步 · 看日晷与节气

Step 2 · Meet the sundial

导航右侧是日晷：公历日期、当前二十四节气、一句节气诗。想换一季风景，点日晷打开季节选择——整站色调、诗句、特效会跟着换季。像把窗外的树摇成另一种绿。

On the right of the nav sits a sundial: calendar date, today’s solar term, and a short verse. Tap it to change season—colours, poems, and effects shift together, as if the trees outside changed their green.

游

第三步 · 沿导航漫游

Step 3 · Wander the nav

文 / Writing、影 / Visual、物 / Craft、数 / Digital，再往下是摄影「四时·流转之境」。卡片与展区多可点击展开——请慢慢点，像翻一本不愿一次读完的书。

Writing, Visual, Craft, Digital, then photography—“Seasons through the Lens.” Cards and galleries open on tap. Go slowly, like a book you refuse to finish in one sitting.

音

第四步 · 在「续音」留下可能

Step 4 · Leave a possible echo

页末「续音」是联系方式。山高水长，愿有回响——若你因这封投递邮件而来，那一声回响，或许就是我们下一次对话的开始。

“续音 / Echo” at the end holds contact details. Across mountains and rivers, may words still answer—if you came from an application email, that answer might begin our next conversation.

Section map: Five kinds of light in one scroll

What you will find when you unfold

文以载心

Writing — the window through which I see

文学创作、评论与双语书写。有英文小说《The Unanswered Call》的多次结构修订，也有远读《都柏林人》的研究足迹——文字是我认识世界的第一种方式。

Creative writing, criticism, bilingual prose. The English novella The Unanswered Call (many structural revisions) and distant-reading work on Dubliners—words were my first way of knowing the world.

流影拾微

Visual & Layout — a quiet conversation

排版、公众号编辑、短视频与视觉叙事。我想让「沉默的空隙」也被设计——标题与留白像呼吸。

Layout, editorial design for WeChat, short video and visual narrative. I design the silent gaps too—titles and white space as breath.

寄情于物

Craft — gentle footnotes to time

书法、染卡、书签、折扇与明信片；还有可互动的「摇签」与垂幕展陈。物很小，却是时间的温柔脚注——我做过「醉花阴」主题文创售卖，也懂得一枚书签如何成为记忆的把手。

Calligraphy, dye cards, bookmarks, fans and postcards; an interactive lot-drawing tube and curtain gallery. Small objects as gentle footnotes to time—I once designed and sold “醉花阴” stationery, and know how a bookmark can be a handle for memory.

数境人文

Digital Humanities — mountains emerge within data

当文学遇见数据：R 语言量化分析、可视化，以及 Vibe Coding 人机共创实验。这座网站本身，就是「数境人文」落地的最大证物。

When literature meets data: quantitative analysis in R, visualisation, and AI-assisted “vibe coding.” This website itself is the largest proof of that practice.

四时·流转之境

Seasons through the Lens

春苔生，夏影长，秋叶落，冬窗寂。校园与四季的摄影，按季节切换浏览——光在四季里留下脚印，我替它一一拾起。

Spring moss, summer shade, autumn leaves, winter windows. Campus and seasonal photography, browsable by season—light left footprints; I picked them up one by one.

05 · Craft

心血细节：那些你不一定第一眼看见的针脚

Craft: Stitches you may not see at first glance

Why this site took so many midnights

Why this site took so many midnights

若只看截图，它像一座漂亮的站；若走近，会发现许多「为一个人的阅读感受」而抠的细节。我愿意把它们写出来——不是炫技，而是想诚实地说：这是我一点点养大的园子。

In a screenshot it looks merely pretty; up close, it is full of stitches made for one reader’s feeling. I list them not to show off, but to say honestly: this is a garden I raised by hand.

  • 双语同屏：不是机翻贴两行，而是中英各自成立——中文求意境，英文求可诵读的呼吸（如启封题辞与四季大标题）。
  • Bilingual on one screen: not machine-translated doubles, but two voices that each stand alone—Chinese for atmosphere, English for breath you can speak aloud.
  • 诗句会「下雨」：启封背景漂浮中英诗片；主页藤蔓旁藏着四季诗句。我希望你还没点开栏目，就已经先被语言的天气包围。
  • Poems that weather the page: fragments drift behind the opening letter; seasonal verses hide along the vine—so language surrounds you before any section opens.
  • 节气不是标签：日晷联动真实日期与二十四节气诗，让「今天」参与排版——你若在雨水来访，读到的气质会不同于霜降。
  • Solar terms are not stickers: the sundial ties real dates to the twenty-four terms and their verses, so “today” joins the layout—Rain Water feels unlike Frost’s Descent.
  • 移动端也要能「被点开」：为手机浏览器修过启封遮罩误拦截点击、卡片触控、竖版信纸比例……琐碎，却决定你是否真的进得去。
  • Mobile must truly open: I fixed overlay click-blocking, card taps, portrait letter proportions—tedious work that decides whether you can actually enter.
  • 全流程署名：信息架构、栏目文案、视觉系统、交互、AI 辅助迭代、GitHub Pages 部署——我都在场。AI 是工具，判断与审美是我的。
  • Full-process authorship: IA, copy, visual system, interaction, AI-assisted iteration, GitHub Pages—I was present for all. AI is a tool; judgment and taste are mine.

山高水长，愿有回响。

Across mountains and rivers, may words still answer.

— 续音 · Closing wish

06 · Letter

致打开邮件的你

To you who opened the email

If you are reading this from an application

If you are reading this from an application

你好。若你因一份简历或一封求职信点开这里，谢谢你多给我三分钟。

我是杨雪琪（Romola），港中文英文文学研究硕士在读。我热爱阅读、策展式的内容组织，也愿意把琐碎做成有温度的成品——正如这个网站：它诞生于课堂里对 R 与文本的好奇，长成于无数次「再改一版」的深夜，最终成为一个可以换季、可以启封、可以用中英两种声音说话的作品集。

我相信内容工作需要四种力：把主题串起来的策展力，写出有温度有观点的叙事力，连接人与文化的连接力，以及把脑中创意落地成推文、页面与活动的行动力。四时风物，是我交给你的一份行动力样本——不完美，但真诚，且仍在生长。

「好雨知时节，当春乃发生。」若此刻正是我们可能共事的时节，我很乐意把这份认真，带到下一段具体的工作里。

Hello. If you opened this from a CV or cover email—thank you for three more minutes.

I am Yang Xueqi (Romola), an MA student in English Literary Studies at CUHK. I love reading and curatorial ways of organising content, and I am willing to turn tedious details into warm finished things—as with this site: born from classroom curiosity about R and text, raised through countless “one more revision” nights, grown into a portfolio that changes seasons, unfolds like a letter, and speaks in Chinese and English.

I believe content work asks for four strengths: curatorial sense to thread a theme, narrative warmth with a point of view, connection between people and culture, and action that lands ideas as posts, pages, and events. Four Seasons is a sample of that action—imperfect, sincere, still growing.

“Good rain knows its season.” If this might be our season to work together, I would gladly bring this care to the next concrete task.

杨雪琪 · ROMOLA
