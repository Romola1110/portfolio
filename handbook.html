01 · Origin

缘起：我想亲手造一个完全属于自己的东西

Origin: I wanted something entirely my own

从文本分析，走向一座网站

From text analysis toward a website

它在港中文的研究生课上开始。那时我用 R 语言做《都柏林人》的远读，看情感如何在短篇里起伏。文字第一次以另一种方式出现在我眼前——像地图，像曲线，像可以走进去的结构。我对编程产生了兴趣，也想知道：如果把这种对文本的敏感，放进一个完全按自己审美搭建的网站里，会是什么样子。

It began in a graduate seminar at CUHK. I used R for distant reading of Dubliners, watching how emotion rises and falls across the stories. Text appeared to me in a new form—as maps, as curves, as structures one could walk into. I grew curious about programming, and wondered what it would mean to place that sensitivity to language inside a website built entirely to my own taste.

后来我开始尝试 AI 辅助编程。我想把对美的感知、对文字与创作的理解，一点一点放进页面里——栏目怎么分，颜色怎么落，哪一句诗该停在哪里。我改了很多版，也记得某个晚上，只是为了手机上的启封页看起来更妥帖一点，来来回回调了很久。那不是因为炫技，只是希望它慢一点、轻一点，像溪水一样流到你面前。

Later I tried AI-assisted coding. I wanted to put my sense of beauty and my understanding of writing and design into the pages—how sections are arranged, how colour settles, where a line of poetry should rest. I revised many times. I remember one evening spent adjusting the opening letter on mobile, again and again—not to show off, but to let it arrive slowly, lightly, like a stream coming toward you.

一笺为引，一卷为心；同声相应，与君共寻。

A small scroll of light, offered as an invitation — when kindred voices answer, we walk this way together.

— 网站启封页题辞 · Opening motto

于是有了 Romola · 四时风物（个人原创作品集网站项目）：中英双语、四季主题、节气日晷、文 / 影 / 数 / 物 / 时 各栏目——从创意构思、文案、视觉交互，到 GitHub Pages 上线，是一条我亲手走完的全流程。链接：romola1110.github.io/portfolio

Thus came Romola · Four Seasons (a personal original portfolio website): bilingual, season-themed, with a solar-term sundial and sections for writing, visual work, digital humanities, craft, and time—from concept and copy to interaction and GitHub Pages deployment, a path I walked myself. romola1110.github.io/portfolio

02 · Theme

主题心法：四时风物，让时间成为首页

Theme: Let time itself be the homepage

春信 · 夏风 · 秋意 · 冬藏

Spring news · Summer wind · Autumn tone · Winter rest

春有花信，雨濯尘氛；夏有长风，光动林影；秋有木叶，暮色含金；冬有雪意，万物归藏。导航旁会显示当日节气与一句节气诗；背景里藤蔓与蝴蝶随季节变色；启封页有「时维桃月，序属仲春」一类的时间文案——灵感来自「时维九月，序属三秋」。春落花瓣，夏起风荷，秋飘木叶，冬洒轻雪——四时各有飘落。你也可在启封页点一点，加入这场盛大的飘落。

Spring brings blossom-news, rain washing the dust; summer brings long wind, light moving through leaves; autumn brings falling leaves, dusk gilded in gold; winter brings quiet snow, all things returning to rest. Beside the navigation, today's solar term appears with a short verse. Vines and butterflies shift with the season. The opening letter carries lines like "In the peach month, in mid-spring"—inspired by classical time-phrasing. Petals fall in spring, lotus wind rises in summer, leaves drift in autumn, soft snow in winter—each season has its own descent. On the opening page, you may click and join that falling.

一笺为引，一卷为心；
同声相应，与君共寻。

A small scroll of light, offered as an invitation —
when kindred voices answer, we walk this way together.

诗词 · 漂浮与藤蔓

Poetry · Drifting & vines

启封页背景有中英诗片轻轻漂浮，会随鼠标微微浮动；每次打开，诗句组合都不完全相同。进入主页后，诗词藤蔓沿页面生长，把各栏目 quietly 串联起来——根与枝在，内容如果实垂落。

On the opening page, Chinese and English poem-fragments drift in the background and sway gently with the cursor; each visit brings a slightly different combination. On the main page, poem-vines grow along the scroll, quietly linking the sections—roots and branches holding, content hanging like fruit.

03 · How to wander

漫游指南：请像读一封信那样点开

How to wander: Open it like a letter

从启封，到四季

From unfolding to the four seasons

启

第一步 · 启封

Step 1 · Unfold

进入网站，先停在信纸前。可读题辞，也可点右上角春夏秋冬预览气质，再点「啟封 · Unfold」。你也可以点一点屏幕，加入花瓣、落叶或轻雪的飘落。

You arrive at the letter-paper. Read the motto, preview the seasons with the small dots, then press Unfold. You may also click the screen to join the falling petals, leaves, or snow.

季

第二步 · 切换时间与主题

Step 2 · Change time and theme

点击导航旁的时间入口，可以在春夏秋冬之间切换——色调、诗句、飘落动效都会随之改变。我希望能进一步丰富画面，比如用一棵树从春芽、夏荫、秋叶到冬枝，串联四季的流转。

Tap the time control beside the navigation to move among the four seasons—colours, verses, and falling effects change with you. I hope to enrich this further, perhaps with a single tree passing from spring buds to summer shade, autumn leaves, and winter branches.

游

第三步 · 沿导航往下

Step 3 · Follow the navigation down

文、影、数、物，再往下是「时」。依次点开查看即可。

Writing, Visual, Digital Humanities, Craft, then Time. Open each section as you go down.

回

第四步 · 在「续音」留下回响

Step 4 · Leave an echo at the end

页末「续音」是联系方式。山高水长，愿有回响——若你因这封信而来，那一声回响，或许就是我们下一次对话的开始。

Echo at the end holds contact details. Across mountains and rivers, may words still answer—if you came from this letter, that answer may begin our next conversation.

The English novella The Unanswered Call; undergraduate thesis on Ulysses and stream of consciousness; comparative and classical literature papers; editorial reflections; selected translations—writing is my first way of knowing the world.

课程展示排版、公众号推送、视频脚本与剪辑、商场与公共宣传的视觉物料——我关注留白与节奏，也关注画面如何替文字说话。

Course layouts, WeChat editorials, video scripts and edits, visual materials for mall and public campaigns—I care about white space and rhythm, and how images speak for words.

Calligraphy, dye cards, bookmarks, fans and postcards; a lot-drawing tube and curtain gallery—small objects, shapes time has kept.

Photography from recent years. The gear may not be professional, but I trust the gaze and taste are my own—you are welcome to see how the seasons turn in my eyes.

05 · Craft

心血：那些你不一定第一眼看见的

Craft: What you may not see at first glance

一座秘密花园，慢慢栽培

A secret garden, slowly tended

若只看截图，它或许只是一页好看的网站；走近一些，才会看见许多为阅读感受而留的细节。我把这里当作一座精心栽培的秘密花园——诗词藤蔓是根与枝叶，各栏目的内容如果实，被悄悄串联起来。

In a screenshot it may look merely beautiful; step closer and many details made for reading begin to appear. I think of it as a secret garden, slowly tended—poem-vines as roots and branches, section contents as fruit, quietly linked together.

  • 双语各成一体：中文重意境，英文重可诵读的呼吸；启封题辞与四季标题都分别写过。
  • Two languages, each whole: Chinese for atmosphere, English for breath you can speak; opening motto and season headlines written separately.
  • 诗词漂浮与藤蔓：启封页诗片随鼠标轻动，每次打开略有不同；主页藤蔓藏四季诗句，先于栏目与你相遇。
  • Drifting poems and vines: fragments sway on the opening page, slightly different each visit; vine-poems on the main scroll meet you before the sections open.
  • 节气与时间：真实日期联动二十四节气与节气诗，「今天」会参与这一页的气质。
  • Solar terms and time: real dates tie to the twenty-four terms and their verses; "today" joins the mood of the page.
  • 移动端：修过启封遮罩拦截点击、卡片触控、竖版信纸比例——琐碎，却决定能否真的进入。
  • Mobile: overlay click-blocking, card taps, portrait letter proportions—small work that decides whether you can truly enter.
  • 纯原创、从零到一：没有借鉴任何网站模板，也不是套模板改结构——信息架构、文案、视觉、交互、AI 辅助迭代与 GitHub Pages 部署，全程由我完成。
  • Original, from zero: no borrowed template, no restyled theme—information architecture, copy, visuals, interaction, AI-assisted iteration, and GitHub Pages deployment, all done by me.
  • 四季签筒：不同季节的签筒样式不同；摇签时筒底纹样也会旋转——为此调试了很多次。
  • Seasonal lot tubes: each season has its own tube design; the base pattern rotates when you draw—tuned through many trials.
  • 垂帘展陈：摇签下方是我临时起意的设计——可随机抽取，也可逐一展阅。图片全部来自我的手写、手绘画与染卡，绝无网图；更多可见我的小红书账号。
  • Curtain gallery: below the lot tube, a design that came in a flash—you may draw at random or view one by one. Every image is from my handwriting, hand-painting, or dye cards—never stock. More on my Xiaohongshu account.
  • 蝴蝶引路：页面上有两只互相追逐的蝴蝶，也是 quietly 的引路人。
  • Butterfly guides: two butterflies chase each other across the page—quiet guides as well.

06 · Letter

致打开信的你

To you who opened this letter

若你正从一封投递信而来

If you came from an application letter

你好。若你因一份简历或一封求职信点开这里，谢谢你多给我三分钟。

我是杨雪琪（Romola），港中文英文文学研究硕士在读。我热爱阅读、写作、创意、设计与策划，也喜欢文字，也喜欢视觉艺术——未必像设计或美术专业那样系统，但我有自己的审美与风格。这个网站诞生于课堂里对 R 与文本的好奇，在无数次「再改一版」里慢慢长大，最终成为一个有生命、能交互、会倾听也会说话的作品集。

我相信内容工作需要四种力：把主题串起来的策展力，写出有温度有观点的叙事力，连接人与文化的连接力，以及把脑中创意落地成推文、页面与活动的行动力。四时风物，是我交给你的一份行动力样本——不完美，但真诚，且仍在生长。

「好雨知时节，当春乃发生。」若此刻正是我们可能共事的时节，我很乐意把这份认真，带到下一段具体的工作里。

Hello. If you opened this from a CV or cover letter—thank you for three more minutes.

I am Yang Xueqi (Romola), an MA student in English Literary Studies at CUHK. I love reading, writing, creative work, design, and planning; I love words and visual art alike—not with the systematic training of a design or fine arts degree, perhaps, but with my own sense of taste and style. This site was born from classroom curiosity about R and text, grew through countless revisions, and became a portfolio that lives, interacts, listens, and speaks.

I believe content work asks for four strengths: curatorial sense to thread a theme, narrative warmth with a point of view, connection between people and culture, and action that lands ideas as posts, pages, and events. Four Seasons is a sample of that action—imperfect, sincere, still growing.

"Good rain knows its season." If this might be our season to work together, I would gladly bring this care to the next concrete task.

杨雪琪 · ROMOLA
